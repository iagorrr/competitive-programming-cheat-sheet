\subsection{Sophie Germain Identity}

The Sophie Germain Identity is a factorization formula for the sum of two fourth powers:

\begin{equation}
    a^4 + 4b^4 = (a^2 + 2b^2 + 2ab)(a^2 + 2b^2 - 2ab)
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Verification}

The identity can be verified by expanding the right-hand side:

\begin{align*}
    &(a^2 + 2b^2 + 2ab)(a^2 + 2b^2 - 2ab) \\
    &= (a^2 + 2b^2)^2 - (2ab)^2 \\
    &= a^4 + 4a^2b^2 + 4b^4 - 4a^2b^2 \\
    &= a^4 + 4b^4
\end{align*}

\subsubsection{Alternative Forms}

The identity can also be written as:

\begin{equation}
    a^4 + 4b^4 = (a^2 + 2ab + 2b^2)(a^2 - 2ab + 2b^2)
\end{equation}

Or in terms of complex numbers:

\begin{equation}
    a^4 + 4b^4 = (a^2 + 2b^2 + 2abi)(a^2 + 2b^2 - 2abi)
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Applications}

The Sophie Germain Identity is useful for:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Factoring expressions of the form $x^4 + 4y^4$
    \item Proving that certain numbers are composite
    \item Solving Diophantine equations
    \item Number theory problems involving fourth powers
\end{itemize}
