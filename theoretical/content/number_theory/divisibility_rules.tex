\subsection{Divisibility rules}

\subsubsection{7}

To check whether a number is divisible by 7, follow this rule:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Take the number in question.
  \item Remove the last digit (units) of the number.
  \item Double the value removed in the previous step.
  \item Subtract the doubled value from the remaining number.
  \item If the result of the subtraction is divisible by 7, the original number is divisible by 7.
\end{enumerate}

Example:

Suppose we want to check whether the number 413 is divisible by 7.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Remove the last digit (3) and double it, obtaining 6.
  \item Subtract 6 from the remaining number (41 - 6 = 35).
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{11}

\[
n \text{ is divisible by 11} \iff \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{2i-1} - \sum_{i=1}^{j} a_{2i} \text{ is divisible by 11}
\]

where \(a_{i}\) is the \(i\)-th digit of the number \(n\), \(k\) is the number of odd-position digits, and \(j\) is the number of even-position digits.

Example:

Suppose we want to check whether the number \(n = 7923\) is divisible by 11.

\[
k = 2, \quad j = 2
\]

\[
\text{Sum of odd-position digits: } 7 + 3 = 10
\]

\[
\text{Sum of even-position digits: } 9 + 2 = 11
\]

\[
\text{Subtraction: } 10 - 11 = -1
\]

Since \(-1\) is not divisible by 11, the number \(7923\) is not divisible by 11.

\subsubsection{13}

\begin{equation}
    13 | x \equiv 13 | 4 \cdot (x\%10) + \lfloor x/10 \rfloor
\end{equation}

In other words, 13 divides $x$ if four times the last digit added to the number without that digit is divisible by 13.

\subsubsection{17}

\begin{equation}
    17 | x \equiv 17 |  \lfloor x/10 \rfloor - 5 \cdot (x \% 10)
\end{equation}

In other words, 17 divides $x$ if the difference between the number without its last digit and five times that last digit is divisible by 17.

\subsubsection{19}

\begin{equation}
    19 | x \equiv 19 | \lfloor x / 10 \rfloor + 2 \cdot (x \mod 10)
\end{equation}

In other words, 19 divides $x$ if twice the last digit of $x$ added to the remaining number of $x$ is divisible by 19.
