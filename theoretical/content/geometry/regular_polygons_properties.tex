\subsection{Regular Polygons Properties}

A regular polygon has all sides of equal length and all interior angles equal.

\subsubsection{Angles}

For a regular polygon with $n$ sides:

\textbf{Exterior angle:}
\begin{equation}
    \theta_{\text{ext}} = \frac{360°}{n}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Interior angle:}
\begin{equation}
    \theta_{\text{int}} = 180° - \theta_{\text{ext}} = \frac{(n-2) \times 180°}{n}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Relationship}

The interior and exterior angles at each vertex are supplementary:

\begin{equation}
    \theta_{\text{int}} + \theta_{\text{ext}} = 180°
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Sum of Angles}

The sum of all interior angles of an $n$-sided polygon is:

\begin{equation}
    S_{\text{int}} = (n-2) \times 180°
\end{equation}

The sum of all exterior angles is always:

\begin{equation}
    S_{\text{ext}} = 360°
\end{equation}
